Sunny Land
----------

Description: pixel art platformer asset pack (tileset, character/enemy sprites, sound,
title/instruction screens) used by this activity's shipped levels.

Copyright: Ansimuz.

License: distributed under a public-domain / CC0-style license, as stated on the pack's
own itch.io page (author: Ansimuz). No local copy of the license text is bundled here;
the itch.io page is the authoritative source of the exact terms.

Local changes: only the files actually used by the plugin were kept under assets/; no
pixel-level edits were made to the original art. Two additional frames were hand-drawn
for this plugin and packed into atlas-props.png/.json alongside the original art:
"sign-info" (exclamation mark) and "sign-checkpoint" (paw print), replacing a skull icon
that had no relation to either the readable-sign or checkpoint function. Both reuse the
original sign post's wood palette and silhouette; only the icon area was redrawn.
