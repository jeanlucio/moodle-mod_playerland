# 🎓 Educational Purpose

PlayerLand is designed to:

* Turn a formative multiple-choice question into an in-game obstacle a student actively seeks
  out, rather than a form to submit
* Tie a question directly to the short explanation that precedes it, so practice and instruction
  sit next to each other in the same level instead of two disconnected activities
* Reward genuine, server-verified progress — the grade and the exit condition are both computed
  from what was actually answered correctly, never from a client-reported number
* Let a struggling student re-read a mini-lesson as many times as needed without any penalty,
  since lesson blocks are never "spent"
* Reach younger and game-oriented learners through a familiar platformer format, without
  requiring any gaming literacy beyond arrow keys and a jump button

Suitable for:

* Any course wanting a short, replayable content-review activity alongside formal quizzes
* Teachers who want to author their own short explanations tied directly to the questions that
  follow them
* Younger student audiences, or any course looking for a lower-pressure, exploratory alternative
  to a traditional quiz
